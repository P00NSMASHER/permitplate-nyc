1. The service

PermitPlate NYC is a founder-curated business research service operated by Jamison Perkins in Pennsylvania. It reviews selected public restaurant, building, and licensing records and sends members a short brief of projects that may be worth researching.

The membership covers one vendor category and one NYC territory chosen at checkout. Each weekly brief contains up to 10 matching signals. If nothing credible matches, the weekly brief will say so.

2. Subscription and billing

The membership costs $79 USD per month and renews automatically until canceled. Stripe provides checkout, receipts, payment processing, tax handling shown at checkout, and the customer billing portal. PermitPlate does not store full card numbers.

You may cancel future renewals through the Stripe customer portal or by contacting support. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period. The refund policy is part of these terms.

3. Delivery

The first brief is prepared within five business days after successful payment. Later briefs are prepared weekly and sent to the checkout email. Delivery depends on accurate checkout information and a working email address.

If you request the Starter Snapshot, the first brief may separately label up to 10 still-active records first seen during the seven days before checkout. They are not presented as post-subscription changes.

4. Public-record limits

Public records can be late, incomplete, duplicated, corrected, or superseded. Names and addresses may not match cleanly across sources. PermitPlate may leave out an unclear record or revise a conclusion when better evidence appears.

A signal does not prove purchase intent, an opening date, a future sale, or that a business will open. PermitPlate does not guarantee exclusive leads, complete coverage, daily volume, accuracy, replies, sales, or revenue. Verify the official record before outreach.

5. Responsible business use

You may use the brief for lawful internal business research and sales planning. You are responsible for your outreach, identity disclosures, consent, opt-out, suppression, and other legal duties.

Do not use PermitPlate to imply government affiliation, harass anyone, infer sensitive personal traits, send unlawful bulk messages, identify private people, resell the service, or publish the briefs as your own database.

6. Ownership and feedback

Government source records remain subject to their source terms. PermitPlate owns its selection, explanations, formatting, software, and brand. You may share individual source links, but not copy or redistribute the paid brief as a competing product.

If you send feedback, PermitPlate may use it to improve the service without changing the underlying public record.

PermitPlate may pause a source, omit unsafe or ambiguous material, or change the service when needed for quality, security, or law. Material membership changes will be communicated before the next renewal when practical.

PermitPlate may suspend or end access for nonpayment, abuse, unlawful use, security risk, or a serious breach of these terms.
