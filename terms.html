1. The service

PermitPlate NYC summarizes selected public administrative records as possible restaurant-opening timing signals. Reports may include a public business name, address, phone number, cuisine, record stage, record date, source identifier, vendor-fit tags, and a transparent sorting score. PermitPlate does not verify that a business is open, will open, needs a vendor, or will buy anything.
