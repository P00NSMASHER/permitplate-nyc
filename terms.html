Terms of Service

Effective September 18, 2026.

1. The service

PermitPlate NYC summarizes selected public administrative records as possible restaurant-opening timing and vendor-prioritization signals. Reports may include public business information, lifecycle stage, source identifiers/links, deterministic vendor-category scores, Why Now, Purchase Window, Watch Next, Confidence, and a separation between observed facts and PermitPlate inference. PermitPlate does not verify that a business is open, will open, needs a vendor, or will buy anything.

2. Sources and limitations

DOHMH is the core restaurant source. PermitPlate may also use current/relevant NY State Liquor Authority pending-license records and NYC DOB build/permit records when strict matching rules pass. Not every signal is multi-source. Public records may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, changed, or corrected.

3. Scores

PermitPlate's POS/payments, Insurance, Equipment, Hood/Fire, Waste, Pest, Linen, and Distribution scores are deterministic prioritization aids. They are not purchase probabilities, purchase-intent predictions, opening probabilities, credit assessments, endorsements, or guarantees.

4. Subscription

The subscription is $79 per month and renews until canceled. There is no setup fee. Checkout, applicable transaction-level tax handling, and subscription management are provided through Stripe.

5. Delivery baseline and historical context

The normal feed begins at the subscriber's subscription baseline and is intended to contain post-baseline new or materially changed signals. On first fulfillment only, PermitPlate may separately label up to 10 still-active records first detected in the prior seven days as Starter Snapshot — active before you joined, with original detection dates shown. Older backlog is not represented as new daily intelligence.

6. Delivery format

Qualifying nonempty digests may include email plus a CRM-ready CSV when technically supported. Source IDs/links are provided to support independent verification. Some days may have no qualifying signals.
