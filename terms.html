Terms of Service

Effective September 19, 2026.

1. The service

PermitPlate NYC is a business research service that summarizes selected public administrative records as possible timing, change and vendor-prioritization signals. The current paid product focuses on NYC restaurant-related public records. Reports may include public business information, lifecycle stage, source identifiers/links, vendor-category scores, What Changed, Why Now, Watch Next, confidence labels, and a separation between observed facts and PermitPlate inference.

2. Public-source and matching limitations

Public records may be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, changed, corrected or superseded. Matching across sources can be uncertain. PermitPlate may suppress ambiguous matches and may later revise an inference when better public evidence appears. A source-linked record does not verify that a business is open, will open, needs a vendor, or will buy anything.

3. Scores and confidence labels

Scores and labels are deterministic research aids. They are not purchase probabilities, purchase-intent predictions, opening probabilities, credit assessments, endorsements or guarantees. Identity confidence and evidence strength are distinct from commercial priority.

4. Subscription

The current self-serve subscription is $79 per month and renews until canceled. There is no setup fee. Checkout, applicable transaction-level tax handling, and subscription management are provided through Stripe.

5. Delivery baseline and material changes

The normal feed begins at the subscriber's subscription baseline and is intended to contain post-baseline new or materially changed signals. An existing entity may reappear when later public evidence materially changes its stage, corroboration, relevance or status. On first fulfillment only, PermitPlate may separately label up to 10 still-active records first detected in the prior seven days as Starter Snapshot — active before you joined. Older backlog is not represented as new daily intelligence.

6. Custom pilots

Founder-reviewed custom territory or category pilots may be offered separately. Scope, price, data sources, delivery format and any market-specific limitations must be agreed before work begins. The existence of a pilot does not imply production coverage for that market.

7. Customer feedback

PermitPlate may record explicit business-workflow feedback such as Investigate, Watch, Irrelevant or Already knew to improve customer-specific filtering, suppressions and product quality. Feedback does not alter the underlying public-source record.

8. Cancellation and refunds

Customers may manage or cancel their subscription securely through Stripe or contact support. The current voluntary first-payment refund policy and treatment of billing errors are described on the Refunds & Cancellation page.

9. Business use

The service is offered for lawful professional and business use. Customers are responsible for outreach, consent and opt-out practices, identity disclosures, suppression lists and vendor decisions. Reports may not be used to misrepresent government affiliation, infer sensitive personal traits, harass anyone, send unlawful bulk messages, or attempt to identify private individuals from public business records.

10. No guaranteed outcome

No lead, response, opening, sale, revenue, profit, accuracy level, market coverage level, or uninterrupted availability is guaranteed. Signals are shared research intelligence, not exclusive lead assignments.
