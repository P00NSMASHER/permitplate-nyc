Straight answers

Before you join.

PermitPlate is business research, not a promise that a restaurant will open or buy.

What am I buying?

A weekly, founder-reviewed research brief filtered to one vendor category and one NYC territory. Each item includes an official source and a short explanation.

Will I get a brief every week?

Yes. If no credible item matches that week, the brief will simply say that. We do not pad it with weak records.

Where does the information come from?

Supported NYC and New York public records, including health, building, and liquor-licensing sources. The exact source stays attached to each item.

Are these guaranteed leads?

No. They are research starting points. A public record does not prove purchase intent, an opening date, or a future sale.

How do cancellation and refunds work?

Cancel future renewals through Stripe or support. Your first payment is refundable before your first brief or no-matches note is sent and for seven calendar days after that first message is sent, not merely seven days after checkout.
