Questions, answered clearly.

The short version: PermitPlate helps you notice possible restaurant openings sooner.

What am I buying?

A daily information service that turns public NYC restaurant-opening records into a short list your team can review quickly.

Where does the data come from?

NYC’s official restaurant inspection dataset. Reports include identifiers and source links so you can verify the record yourself.

Does this mean the restaurant will buy from me?

No. These are early signals, not guaranteed sales leads or purchase-intent claims.

What if there are no useful signals today?

There may be no report that day. PermitPlate does not pad reports with stale records just to create volume.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Customers can manage or cancel their subscription securely through Stripe.
