Questions, answered clearly.

Where does the data come from?

NYC DOHMH is the core restaurant source. PermitPlate may also use current NY State Liquor Authority pending-license records and NYC DOB NOW build/permit records when a record is relevant and passes strict address/identity and hospitality rules. Not every signal is multi-source.

What do the scores mean?

PermitPlate produces separate deterministic 0–100 prioritization scores for POS/payments, Insurance, Equipment, Hood/Fire, Waste, Pest, Linen, and Distribution. Scores help sort research; they are not probabilities or purchase-intent predictions.

What counts as new?

The normal feed begins at your subscription baseline. A first fulfillment may separately include up to 10 prior-seven-day active records labeled “Starter Snapshot — active before you joined.” Older backlog is not presented as new.

Does a signal mean the restaurant wants my service?

No. Public records can be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, changed, or corrected. Signals are research/timing indicators, not proof of opening, buying intent, vendor need, or exclusivity.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through Stripe. See the Refunds page for the current first-payment refund policy.
