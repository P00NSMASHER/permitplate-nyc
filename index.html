THE OUTPUT

An Opportunity Brief, not another lead row.

Each useful signal is designed to answer four questions without hiding the source material.

What changed?

The exact public event or stage transition that reopened the record.

Why now?

Why that change is worth reviewing for the selected vendor category.

What proves it?

Observed facts, source IDs/links, match strength, timestamps and confidence.

What next?

The next official milestone worth watching if the opportunity is not ready yet.

PermitPlate keeps Observed Facts separate from PermitPlate Inference. Low-confidence identity matches fail closed instead of being rescued by a high commercial score.

Questions, answered clearly.

PermitPlate is deliberately conservative about what it claims.

Where does the data come from?

For the current NYC restaurant product, DOHMH is the core source. PermitPlate may also use current/relevant NY State Liquor Authority pending-license records and NYC DOB build/permit records when conservative entity/address and hospitality rules pass.

Is PermitPlate a national permit database?

No. The current paid product is NYC restaurant intelligence. The model can support additional public-record markets, but each new source/market requires separate rights, matching, freshness and usefulness validation before customer claims are made.

What do the scores mean?

Scores are deterministic prioritization aids for a specific vendor category. Identity confidence and evidence sufficiency are gating conditions; a weak match is not promoted merely because it looks commercially interesting.

What counts as new?

The normal feed begins at the subscription baseline. A material change to an already-known venue can also reopen it. A first fulfillment may separately include up to 10 recent active records labeled as a starter snapshot; older backlog is not presented as new.

Does a signal mean the business wants my service?

No. A signal is source-linked research intelligence, not evidence of buying intent, an opening guarantee, an exclusive lead or an endorsement.
