The honest answers

Before you subscribe.

Public records are useful precisely because they are evidence—not certainty.

What am I actually buying?

A source-linked research feed filtered to your vendor category and NYC territory. It highlights qualifying applicant, licensing, buildout, pre-permit, and material-change activity. It is not a contact database or a promise of daily volume.

Where does the information come from?

NYC DOHMH is the core restaurant source. Relevant NYC DOB and New York State Liquor Authority evidence may be included when the venue match is strong enough. Every brief keeps the supporting source reference visible.

Does a high score mean the restaurant will buy?

No. Scores prioritize research for a vendor category. They are not purchase probabilities, opening probabilities, credit assessments, endorsements, or guarantees.

What counts as “new”?

“New” means first detected after your subscription baseline, or a later material change to a known venue. Detection time is not represented as a filing date or opening date. An optional starter snapshot is labeled as activity that predates your subscription.

Will I receive something every day?

Not necessarily. PermitPlate sends a brief when qualifying activity is available for your feed. A quiet day should stay quiet instead of being padded with weak or recycled records.

How do cancellation and refunds work?

You can cancel through Stripe or support. Cancellation stops future renewals, and the first subscription payment has a seven-calendar-day refund window under the current policy.
