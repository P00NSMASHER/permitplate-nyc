Common questions.

The short version: PermitPlate helps vendors notice restaurant-opening activity earlier without pretending public records are buying intent.

Where does the data come from?

NYC DOHMH is the core restaurant source. PermitPlate can also use relevant NYC DOB and New York State Liquor Authority records when the match to the venue is strong enough.

Does every public record become a lead?

No. Duplicate, ambiguous, institutional, or weakly matched records can be suppressed. The goal is a useful shortlist, not the largest possible list.

What do the scores mean?

Scores help prioritize research for a specific vendor category. They are not purchase probabilities, credit scores, opening probabilities, or guarantees.

What counts as new?

Your normal feed starts when you subscribe. An existing venue may appear again if a later public record materially changes the opportunity. Older backlog is not presented as new activity.

Does a signal mean the restaurant wants my service?

No. PermitPlate shows public evidence that may make a business worth researching. Your team still decides whether and how to contact it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can manage or cancel through Stripe. See the Refunds page for the current first-payment refund policy.
