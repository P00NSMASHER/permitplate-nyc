# Agent Data Tools x402

Use this service when an agent needs low-cost authoritative public-data lookups or a bounded Pennsylvania vendor-intake workflow decision.

## Endpoints and prices

- /_api/pa-entity-one — $0.001 — Pennsylvania registry best match
- /_api/pa-business — $0.005 — Pennsylvania enriched multi-result search
- /_api/vendor-intake-gate — $0.020 — proceed/human_review vendor-intake decision
- /_api/sec-filings — $0.005 — SEC EDGAR recent filings
- /_api/us-address-geocode — $0.005 — Census address geocoding
- /_api/ofac-sdn-screen — $0.005 — OFAC SDN candidate-name screening
- /_api/domain-rdap — $0.005 — authoritative domain RDAP
- /_api/treasury-average-rates — $0.005 — Treasury monthly average rates

All prices are USDC on Base via x402 v2.

## Selection guidance

Choose /_api/vendor-intake-gate when an agent needs an action decision rather than raw source records.

Choose the underlying raw endpoints when the agent needs source facts directly or wants to perform its own decision policy.

The vendor gate calls its evidence-source logic internally and does not create nested seller-funded x402 purchases.

## Safety and interpretation

OFAC output is candidate-name screening only. A match is not a legal determination; a no-match is not sanctions clearance; ownership analysis under the OFAC 50 Percent Rule is not performed.

PA registry records do not independently prove current good standing, ownership, or authority to contract.

Census geocoding does not prove physical presence or control of an address.

RDAP registration and name alignment do not prove domain ownership or control.

Treasury average-interest-rate data are monthly averages on outstanding debt, not live market yields.

SEC filing metadata are authoritative EDGAR identifiers and dates, not investment advice or a claim about filing contents.

## Payment recovery

If a source fails before a usable result, payment is not settled.

If settlement is unresolved, retry the same request with the same PAYMENT-SIGNATURE rather than generating a fresh authorization.
