Privacy Notice

Effective September 19, 2026.

Information handled

PermitPlate may handle subscriber email, company/profile preferences, territory/category/minimum-score settings, Stripe subscription/payment-status identifiers, delivery state, explicit customer dispositions, support communications, operational logs, and public business-record fields from official sources. PermitPlate does not need to store full card numbers.

Public-source intelligence

PermitPlate processes public administrative records to build source-linked business research signals. Where technically supported, records may be normalized, deduplicated, linked to a stable business/project identity, compared with prior source states, and retained with source IDs, timestamps, revision fingerprints or links so material changes can be audited and rechecked.

Customer feedback

Explicit dispositions such as Investigate, Watch, Irrelevant or Already knew may be stored to personalize filtering and improve product quality. Customer feedback does not alter the underlying source record and is not represented as a public fact.

How information is used

Information is used to provide and secure subscriptions, establish the customer's delivery baseline, personalize and prepare reports, prevent duplicate delivery, detect material changes, maintain source lineage, troubleshoot failures, respond to support, honor suppression/correction requests, and comply with law.

Service providers

PermitPlate uses third-party cloud, payment, hosting, version-control and automation providers—including Google services, Stripe, Netlify, GitHub, and OpenAI-powered systems—to operate the service. These providers may process information necessary to provide and support the service under their applicable terms.

Data minimization

The product is intended to use business-oriented public administrative data and customer account/workflow data. PermitPlate does not need customers to submit passwords, full card numbers, government identifiers, health information, or other sensitive personal information for ordinary use.

Retention

Subscriber, billing-reference, baseline, delivery, feedback, support, source-trace and operational records are retained only as reasonably needed for service operation, accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, backup integrity, source verification, product quality and legal obligations.

Security

Reasonable access controls and least-privilege integrations are used to reduce risk. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Do not send card numbers, passwords, authentication tokens or sensitive personal information by support email.
