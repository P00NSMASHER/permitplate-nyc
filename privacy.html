Privacy Notice

What PermitPlate handles, why it is needed, and how to reach us.

1. What we collect

At checkout, Stripe may collect your email, business name, billing address, tax information, payment method, vendor category, NYC territory, and Starter Snapshot choice. PermitPlate receives the account details and payment-status identifiers needed to provide the membership. PermitPlate does not receive or store your full card number.

We also handle support messages, delivery history, the signal keys already sent to you, and public business records from official sources.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • confirm and manage your subscription;
  • prepare the brief for your category and territory;
  • send the brief and avoid duplicate delivery;
  • answer support, cancellation, and refund requests;
  • secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service; and
  • meet accounting, fraud-prevention, and legal duties.

We do not sell member personal information or use it for third-party behavioral advertising.

3. Service providers

PermitPlate uses providers that process only the information needed for their role. These may include Stripe for checkout and billing, GitHub for website hosting and private operational storage, email and cloud tools for delivery, and OpenAI-powered tools that assist with software or research workflows.

The public website does not run a first-party advertising tracker. GitHub Pages may receive ordinary server logs. When the footer checks DOHMH freshness, your browser requests public metadata from NYC Open Data. Stripe applies its own privacy terms when you open checkout or the billing portal.

4. Public business records

PermitPlate processes public administrative records to create business research signals. We may normalize, compare, deduplicate, and retain source IDs, official URLs, dates, and review notes so a delivered item can be checked and duplicate delivery can be prevented.

5. Retention and security

Account, billing-reference, support, delivery, and source-trace records are kept only as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, backups, and legal duties.

Reasonable access controls and private operational storage are used. No online service can promise perfect security. Do not email card numbers, passwords, authentication tokens, or sensitive personal information.

6. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete applicable account information, or request suppression of a public business record from future briefs. Some records must be retained for billing, fraud prevention, disputes, or law.

Contact jayp19386@gmail.com. The service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children.

7. Changes

This notice may be updated as the service changes. A new effective date will appear at the top. Material changes affecting members will be communicated when practical.
