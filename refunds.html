Refunds & cancellation

Simple terms, with a clear first-payment refund window.

On this pageCancelFirst paymentBilling errorsRequest help

Cancel anytime

Cancel through the secure Stripe customer portal or email support. Cancellation stops the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the current paid period.

Seven-day first-payment refund

Your first PermitPlate subscription payment is eligible for a full refund when you request it within seven calendar days after the charge. You do not need to give a reason.

This voluntary policy does not reduce any longer right required by applicable law. After the seven-day window, correctly billed subscription charges are generally non-refundable unless required by law or agreed in writing.

Billing errors and service failures

Confirmed duplicate charges, charges after an effective cancellation, and other billing errors will be corrected. A material failure to deliver the purchased service will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

Request help

Email jayp19386@gmail.com from the subscriber email. Include the charge date and a short description. Never send card details or passwords.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Your bank controls when the funds appear.
