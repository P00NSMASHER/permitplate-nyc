METHODOLOGY

Transparent signals, not a black box.

PermitPlate uses deterministic public-record rules for detection, scoring, deduplication, and delivery.

Official source

PermitPlate monitors the New York City Department of Health and Mental Hygiene Restaurant Inspection Results dataset. Source freshness is checked before production delivery. Open the official dataset ↗

Records monitored

New applicants: records with the NYC sentinel inspection date indicating the establishment has not yet received a normal inspection. Pre-permit activity: recent records whose official inspection type begins with “Pre-permit.”

Signal score

The score measures timing and public-record completeness. It does not predict purchase intent or opening success.

Scores are capped from 0 to 100. The current qualification threshold is 55.

Business-name quality filter

PermitPlate rejects obvious placeholders and malformed date-like business names while preserving legitimate punctuation- and number-leading DBAs.

Deduplication

Never-inspected applicants use a stable lead key based on CAMIS. Pre-permit records use CAMIS plus inspection date. Delivery state is also tracked per subscriber/lead pair so a previously delivered signal is not resent as new.

Vendor-fit tags

Vendor-fit labels are rule-based convenience tags derived from cuisine and the type of services restaurants commonly require. They are not evidence that a listed business needs or wants any particular vendor.

Freshness and fail-closed behavior

Production delivery checks NYC source metadata before sending. If the source is stale or unavailable, the system is designed to avoid inventing or padding results.

Delivery

Reports are processed daily. Active subscribers receive up to 25 pending qualifying signals in a digest. Some days may have no qualifying new signals. Signals are not exclusive lead assignments.

Limitations

Public records can be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, changed, or corrected. PermitPlate is a research and timing tool. Customers remain responsible for verifying official records and for lawful outreach.
